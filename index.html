Our story

Tidewren started in 2021 in a roller-door warehouse in Brunswick with a 15 kg drum roaster and a rule: roast less, roast often, and tell people exactly where each coffee comes from. The name comes from the little wren that hops along the Merri Creek at low tide, which is where we walk the dog between roasts.

We buy green coffee from importers who name the farm or the washing station, and we publish the farm, the process and the price paid per kilo on every bag.
